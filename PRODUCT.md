# Praxis Product

## Promise

Praxis turns what people learn into what they do. It is the action layer for useful knowledge, not a note warehouse.

## Core user

People who consume useful information and want it to change decisions, behavior, or outcomes: founders, professionals, students, creators, and deliberate learners.

## Core loop

1. Capture a useful fragment quickly.
2. Distill the real insight.
3. Interpret it in personal context.
4. Convert it into an action, habit, experiment, decision rule, or contextual trigger.
5. Resurface it when relevant.
6. Record execution and outcome.
7. Reuse, publish, or discover evidence-backed applied knowledge.

## North-star metric

Applied Insights: insights linked to a real action or meaningful contextual use. Supporting metrics are completion rate, outcome response rate, and reported helpfulness.

## Major product areas

- Today: prioritized actions, Inbox work, habits, experiments, and resurfaced knowledge.
- Inbox: fast captures awaiting interpretation.
- Library: processed private knowledge with full-text and semantic-ready retrieval.
- Actions and outcomes: commitments plus evidence of what happened.
- Ask Praxis: grounded personal or public knowledge answers with citations.
- Explore: public Applied Insights ranked by application and helpfulness.
- Goals, collections, progress, weekly review, notifications, and profiles.
- Chrome/Chromium extension for pages, selections, notes, and YouTube timestamps.

## Product rules

- Private by default. Publication is explicit.
- Human interpretation is required; AI assists rather than replaces thinking.
- Optimize for application, not collection or feed engagement.
- Never invent user metrics, outcomes, sources, or AI evidence.
- Store source metadata and conservative excerpts, not unauthorized complete works.

## Non-goals

No Notion-style document system, generic social feed, 3D knowledge graph, gamification economy, payment simulation, or environmental-awareness claims. Billing, calendar context, push notifications, and a playbook marketplace wait until the core loop proves demand.
