# Praxis — Product Source of Truth

## Product

Praxis is a **Knowledge-to-Action platform**.

## Mission

**Turn what you learn into what you do.**

## Problem

People consume enormous amounts of valuable information but:

- forget it,
- scatter it across platforms,
- fail to revisit it,
- fail to convert it into concrete action,
- fail to follow through,
- and rarely record whether applying the advice actually worked.

## Core future loop

**Capture → Interpret → Decide → Act → Complete → Outcome → Reflect**

The fundamental long-term data/value loop is:

**Knowledge → Interpretation → Action → Commitment → Execution → Outcome → Reflection → New Knowledge**

## Future Insight classification

Captured knowledge may one day be classified as:

- **Remember** — worth retaining and resurfacing.
- **Apply** — worth converting into concrete action.
- **Explore** — worth investigating further before deciding.

## Long-term differentiation

Praxis should eventually optimize for **knowledge applied**, not information consumed.

Potential long-term capabilities include:

- universal knowledge capture,
- personal interpretations,
- action generation,
- reminders,
- outcome tracking,
- contextual resurfacing,
- "You Already Know This" detection,
- weekly growth reviews,
- Ask My Knowledge,
- Personal Growth Graph,
- and eventually an optional public Applied Knowledge Network.

## Important product principle

Praxis is NOT intended to become:

- another generic note-taking app,
- another generic todo application,
- another generic AI chatbot,
- another endless social content feed,
- or another second-brain clone.

The central question is:

> **How can Praxis maximize the probability that useful knowledge actually changes what the user does?**
